Common mistakes to avoid

  • Taking measurements while the patient is moving
  • Using the device on cold fingers
  • Recording numbers before they stabilize
  • Forgetting to clean the device between patients
  • Ignoring symptoms because the oxygen level looks normal
  • Assuming one measurement is enough to diagnose a medical problem

Safety is always the priority

Contact a healthcare provider promptly for SpO₂ of 92% or lower for most people; 88% or lower requires immediate medical attention. Trouble breathing, chest pain, confusion, or bluish lips can signal an emergency regardless of the displayed number.

In a real healthcare setting, follow your supervisor's directions, facility protocols, and the patient's individualized care plan.
